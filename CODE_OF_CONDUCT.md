# Community code of conduct

DevSnapshot welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent, critique ideas
rather than people, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sharing
someone else's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's security advisory channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
with respect for the privacy of everyone involved.
